🩺 Regular Checkups

Regular health checkups can help women understand their health, identify concerns early and get appropriate medical guidance.

Your Health Deserves Attention

Regular healthcare visits give you an opportunity to discuss your health, ask questions and identify problems that may need further evaluation.

The type and frequency of checkups can vary depending on your age, health history, family history, lifestyle and individual needs.

🌷 What Can Be Discussed?

🩺 General Health

Discuss your overall health, symptoms, medicines and any health concerns you may have.

🩸 Blood Health

Your healthcare professional may recommend blood tests when appropriate based on your symptoms and health needs.

❤️ Blood Pressure

Blood pressure can be checked as part of routine healthcare and monitored when necessary.

🌸 Women's Health

Discuss menstrual concerns, reproductive health and other women-specific health questions.

🧠 Mental Wellbeing

Talk about stress, anxiety, low mood, sleep problems or other concerns affecting your wellbeing.

🥗 Lifestyle

Discuss nutrition, physical activity, sleep and other lifestyle factors that may affect your health.

💗 Healthy Checkup Habits: Do & Don't

✅ Do

  • Keep track of important health information.
  • Tell your healthcare professional about ongoing symptoms.
  • Ask questions when you do not understand something.
  • Carry information about medicines you are taking.
  • Follow recommended follow-up appointments.
  • Discuss relevant family health history.
  • Seek medical help when new or concerning symptoms appear.

❌ Don't

  • Do not ignore persistent or unusual symptoms.
  • Do not hide important health information from your doctor.
  • Do not take medicines without appropriate medical advice.
  • Do not postpone necessary follow-up care.
  • Do not feel embarrassed about asking health questions.
  • Do not rely only on online information for diagnosis.
  • Do not assume every symptom is harmless.

👩‍⚕️ When Should You See a Doctor?

You should consider speaking with a healthcare professional when you have a new, persistent or unexplained health concern.

Seek urgent medical care for serious symptoms such as severe chest pain, difficulty breathing, fainting, severe bleeding, sudden weakness or other rapidly worsening symptoms.

Your healthcare professional can decide whether an examination, screening test or further investigation is appropriate for you.
