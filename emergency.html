🛡️ What To Do In An Emergency

Simple steps that can help you stay safer.

📞

Call For Help

If you are in immediate danger, call emergency services or ask someone nearby to help.

📍

Share Your Location

Share your current location with a trusted family member, friend or person you trust.

🏃‍♀️

Move To A Safer Place

If possible, move towards a crowded or safer public place.

👥

Contact Someone You Trust

Tell a trusted person what is happening. You do not have to face the situation alone.

📷

Keep Important Evidence

Where appropriate and safe, preserve messages, documents or other information related to the incident.

💗

Do Not Blame Yourself

Asking for help is a sign of strength. Your safety matters.

💗 You Don't Have To Stay Silent
