💡 Myth vs Fact

❌ Myth

"I should stay silent if someone is troubling me."

✅ Fact

You can seek help from trusted people and appropriate support services.

❌ Myth

"I cannot ask about my legal rights."

✅ Fact

Learning about your rights can help you make informed decisions.

❌ Myth

"I should share my OTP if someone says they are from a bank."

✅ Fact

Never share OTPs or passwords with unknown callers.
