👩‍⚕️ When Should I Seek Help?

If something about your health worries you, talking to a qualified healthcare professional is a good place to start.

You do not have to ignore a health problem or feel embarrassed about asking for help.

  • 🩺 Doctor: Consult a qualified healthcare professional.
  • 🏥 Hospital: Visit a healthcare facility when professional care is needed.
  • 💗 Trusted Person: Talk to someone you trust.
  • 🚨 Emergency: Seek immediate emergency help when there is serious danger.
